## Frozen contract — what "released" means

Both tasks here, and both tasks in `04-02-PLAN.md`, are written against this one sentence:

> A junction row with `invoice_appointments.is_archived = false` is a **live claim** on its
> appointment. A junction row with `is_archived = true` is a **retained historical record** of a
> claim that was released, and it claims nothing.

Consequences, all four of which are asserted somewhere in this phase:

1. At most one live claim may exist per `appointment_id` — enforced by `04-01-T1`'s index.
2. `appointments.billed_price_cents` is non-null **exactly when** a live claim exists, and then it
   equals that row's `billed_amount_cents` — asserted by `04-01-T2`'s two invariants.
3. An appointment with no live claim is offered by both invoice builders — `04-02-T2`.
4. `voidInvoice` is the only writer of the marker — `04-02-T1`.

Nothing anywhere keys on `invoices.status` to decide whether an appointment is spoken for. See
`04-CONTEXT.md §5` AMD-3 for why the status predicate was dropped from REQ-022's acceptance
wording.

### Changed-risk surface

A table-wide UNIQUE is being replaced by a partial one on a live database's core billing junction,
and the script that reports whether the billing cache is consistent is being narrowed at the same
time. Three failure modes are worth hunting specifically:

1. **A predicate that does not partition.** If `is_archived` were nullable, rows with NULL would
   satisfy neither half and silently escape uniqueness. It is `NOT NULL DEFAULT false`
   (`20260918120000:142-145`) — confirm that in the catalogue, do not take it on trust.
2. **D-25's half-narrowing.** One invariant tightened and not the other is worse than neither. The
   diff makes this checkable in ten seconds; check it.
3. **A silent backfill.** The migration must change zero rows under the OQ-V1 default. Prove it by
   count, not by reading the file.
