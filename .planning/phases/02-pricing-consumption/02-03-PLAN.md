### What to do

Inside the `"invariant"` CTE, the two cache invariants must assert exactly this — no invoice
status anywhere, no join to `invoices`:

1. **`'appointment cache disagrees with its junction row'`** — count rows of `appointments` joined
   to `invoice_appointments` on `appointment_id`, where
   `a.billed_price_cents IS DISTINCT FROM ia.billed_amount_cents`. **Remove** the
   `JOIN "public"."invoices" "i" ON ("i"."id" = "ia"."invoice_id")` and the
   `WHERE "i"."status" <> 'void'` predicate presently at **:93-94**, leaving the
   `IS DISTINCT FROM` comparison as the sole `WHERE` clause.
2. **`'appointment cached a price with no junction row'`** — unchanged from its current form
   (**:97-100**): `billed_price_cents IS NOT NULL` AND `NOT EXISTS` any `invoice_appointments` row
   for that appointment. It takes no status filter either. Confirm it has not been narrowed; if it
   has, undo that too.

The two together are one **iff** — a cache value exists exactly when a junction row claims the
appointment, and then it matches — and under Branch A every junction row is a claim, because none
is ever released. Narrowing either one alone opens a hole in the pair; that is why AMD-2 specifies
them together.

Everything else in the file is untouched: the other three invariants, all four sections, the
labels, the ordering, the `UNION ALL` single-statement structure and the shared
label/subject/detail/before/after/delta shape. The file must remain read-only.
